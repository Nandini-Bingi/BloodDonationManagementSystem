How BloodBridge Works

Donate blood in four simple steps and become someone's hero.

1

Register Yourself

Create your donor profile by providing your personal details, blood group, and contact information.

2

Eligibility Check

Ensure you meet the required eligibility criteria before scheduling your blood donation.

3

Donate Blood

Visit a partnered hospital or blood donation camp and donate safely under medical supervision.

4

Save Lives

Your donation helps patients in emergencies, surgeries, and life-saving treatments.
